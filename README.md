# PaperKnife+

A privacy-first PDF utility for Android. Works 100% offline - no internet, no trackers.

[![Download](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-APK-green?style=for-the-badge)](https://github.com/potatameister/PaperKnifePlus/releases/latest)
[![Join](https://img.shields.io/badge/Join-Discord-blue?style=for-the-badge)](https://discord.gg/dSPXHWtHAX)
[![Follow](https://img.shields.io/badge/Follow-X-black?style=for-the-badge)](https://x.com/potatameister)

---

## Features

- Merge & Split PDFs
- Password protect your PDFs  
- Convert Images to PDF
- Export PDF pages as Images (ZIP)
- Rotate and rearrange pages

All processing happens on your device. Your files never leave your phone.
